# Acme Cloud

A Acme Cloud is a fictional software company that provides cloud infrastructure and developer tools.

The company was founded in 2018 and is headquartered in Lisbon, Portugal.

Acme Cloud focuses on helping software teams deploy and operate applications without managing physical servers.

The company has three main teams:

- Platform Engineering: responsible for infrastructure and deployment systems.
- Developer Experience: responsible for developer tools and documentation.
- Customer Support: responsible for helping customers troubleshoot problems.

The Acme Cloud platform is available in three regions:

- Europe
- North America
- Asia Pacific

The company uses a subscription-based pricing model. Customers can choose between the Starter, Professional, and Enterprise plans.
